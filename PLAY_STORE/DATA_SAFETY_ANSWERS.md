# Play Console > App content > Data safety - answers for Bijnor 3D Battle Royal & Survival Game

The game works fully offline: it has no internet permission, no ad library, no analytics and no server. All game data stays on the device.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **No** |
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | Not applicable (nothing is collected or sent) |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? | **Yes**, in the game: Settings > ACCOUNT > DELETE ALL MY DATA (or Android Settings > Apps > Clear storage). Nothing is stored outside the device. |

## Other App content declarations

| Section | Answer |
|---|---|
| Ads | **No** |
| Advertising ID | **No** (the manifest declares only `VIBRATE`) |
| Target audience | 13+ (not a Families app) |
| In-app purchases | None |

The privacy policy (docs/privacy-policy.html, generated by tools/make_privacy_policy.py) says the same.
